About

Rishi Kiran.

Actor · AD

A storyteller who knows the set from every angle.

Rishi Kiran is a Chennai-based actor whose work spans television, commercials, web and vertical series, short films and theatre. His negative lead in Kalaignar TV's Rudhra showed a clear, unsettling screen presence, and his commercial work includes TVS Jupiter alongside MS Dhoni.

He trained in theatre with Jaya Rao Master at Actors Workstation, known for his work on Jai Bhim and Madras, and works behind the camera as an assistant director, junior artist coordinator, editor and production manager, including projects with Suresh Mari and Manikandan.

He brings authenticity, discipline and a distinctive energy to every project, and treats each role as a chance to learn and leave a lasting impression.
